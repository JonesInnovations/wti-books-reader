Privacy Policy

This app is an internal tool used only by Wyatt Trapp Inc. to read its own QuickBooks Online company file.

  • It is read-only: it never writes to QuickBooks.
  • Data is read to the company's own computer and stays there. Nothing is stored in the cloud.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • The app's credentials are kept in Windows Credential Manager on that computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in QuickBooks.
